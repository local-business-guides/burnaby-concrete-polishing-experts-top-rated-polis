Understand driveway polishing in Burnaby

A concrete driveway can be polished to change the look and feel of its existing surface. It is not the same job as building a new driveway or replacing a damaged slab. At Burnaby Concrete Polishing Experts, our focus is polished concrete flooring, so start by asking whether polishing fits the condition and intended use of your driveway.

Polishing works with the concrete already in place. The result depends on the slab and the preparation needed, so a conversation about the surface comes before deciding on a finish. If you need new concrete poured, a substantial repair, or a specific drainage change, confirm that the provider handles that work rather than assuming it is part of polishing.
