A concrete pad may serve as a base for outdoor equipment, a work area, or another practical use around a property. If the pad is already in place, polishing may be worth discussing when the goal is to change its surface appearance. Polishing does not create a new pad or correct every problem with the underlying concrete.

What the service can involve

For a suitable existing surface, polishing generally involves preparing the concrete and working its surface toward the finish being discussed. The precise steps depend on the pad, its current condition, and the result requested. A contractor should explain the planned preparation and what the finished surface is expected to look like.

Key facts to discuss include:

  • Whether the existing pad is appropriate for polishing.
  • What surface preparation is planned and included.
  • Which visible conditions polishing may address, and which it may not.
  • How access, pad size, and the chosen finish affect the work.

There is no single timing that fits every pad. The schedule depends on its size and condition, required preparation, access, and the finish. Cost varies according to the same project details and any additional work, so request a scope-specific explanation rather than relying on a general figure.

A few terms can help when reviewing a proposal. “Surface preparation” means getting the concrete ready for the planned work. “Grinding” is a mechanical step that can remove or smooth surface material. “Polished concrete” describes a concrete surface worked to a selected finish. Ask what each term means for your particular pad.

Concrete Pad Contractor Burnaby: common questions

Can you polish a concrete pad that is already installed?
An existing pad may be considered for polishing, depending on its condition and the finish you want. Contact us with details so we can discuss whether the service fits your project.
Is polishing the same as installing a new concrete pad?
No. Polishing works on an existing concrete surface, while a new pad requires concrete installation. Confirm which type of work a provider offers before requesting a proposal.
What affects the timing and cost of pad polishing?
The pad's size and condition, access, preparation needs, and selected finish can all affect the project. Ask for timing and cost information based on the actual pad and scope.
